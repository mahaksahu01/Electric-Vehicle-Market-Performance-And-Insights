Key insights

What the analysis of ev_market_test.csv shows.

~2%

No single leader

The top three manufacturers (SurgeWorks, CurrentMobility, ElektraMobility) make up only about 2% of total sales.

4%

Similar regional demand

Europe is highest at 112.5M units and Africa lowest at 108.3M, a gap of about 4%.

66%

Two leading batteries

NMC (33.8%) and LFP (32.1%) make up about two-thirds of models. Average range is about 324 km whichever type is used.

0.28

Weak price and range link

The correlation between price and range is low, so paying more does not reliably buy a longer range.

83–88M

Steady yearly sales

Annual sales stay in a narrow band from 2018 to 2026, with no sharp growth or drop.
